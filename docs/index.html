How it works

From chat to a finished clip

Three steps inside Telegram. No separate app to install.

1

Send your idea

Message the Telegram bot @ZhongZena_bot. Send a plain text idea, or one product photo plus a short motion brief. Personal clips can use a guided setup or a full prompt.

2

Pick duration and ratio

Choose 5 to 15 seconds. Choose vertical 9:16 or landscape 16:9. Output is 768P, generated by AI.

3

Receive the clip

The finished clip arrives in the chat. Pay with DuitNow QR or FPX bank transfer through ToyyibPay. A DuitNow QR fee of RM1 (or 1%) is shown at checkout.

The bot menus are in Bahasa Malaysia and English.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I order a video?

Message the Telegram bot @ZhongZena_bot. Send a plain text idea for a personal clip. Business customers send one product photo plus a short motion brief. Personal video creation can be a guided setup or a full prompt.

How do I pay?

Packs are priced in Malaysian ringgit. Pay with DuitNow QR or FPX bank transfer through ToyyibPay. A DuitNow QR fee of RM1 (or 1%) is shown at checkout.

Are the videos labelled as AI-generated?

Yes. Every delivered video is labelled AI-generated. Every clip on this site was made with AI and is labelled AI-generated. The store asks buyers to keep that disclosure.

Is exact text inside the video guaranteed?

No. Exact text rendered inside a video is not guaranteed.

Can I send a product photo?

Yes. Business customers send one product photo plus a short motion brief. GENVID turns that into a short moving product clip. Product or logo fidelity in the beta image-to-video path is best effort.

What languages does the bot support?

The bot is bilingual. Menus are in Bahasa Malaysia and English.
