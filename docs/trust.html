Labels

AI disclosure

Every GENVID clip is AI-generated. This applies to personal videos and business product videos alike.

Every delivered video carries an AI-generated label. We ask buyers to keep that disclosure. Do not remove or hide it.

Every clip on this site was made with AI and is labelled AI-generated.

Beta

What the service can and cannot promise

You get a short AI video, 5 to 15 seconds, vertical 9:16 or landscape 16:9, at 768P.

Exact text rendered inside a video is not guaranteed. Product or logo fidelity in the beta image-to-video path is best effort. Motion artefacts are normal. Results are delivered as-is in beta.

Rules

Acceptable use

Do not order content that includes:

  • deepfakes or impersonation of real people without consent
  • political or medical misinformation
  • harassment
  • illegal products or services
  • anything that breaks Malaysian law or TikTok rules

We can refuse or cancel such orders.

Ringgit

Payments and refunds

Packs are prepaid credits in Malaysian ringgit (RM). There is no subscription. Credits do not expire.

Credit use:

  • 1 credit = one 5 or 6 second clip
  • 10 second clip = 2 credits
  • 15 second clip = 3 credits

Packs:

  • RM5 - 1 credit
  • RM12 - 3 credits
  • RM40 - 10 credits plus 2 bonus credits
  • RM90 - 25 credits plus 5 bonus credits

Pay with DuitNow QR or FPX bank transfer through ToyyibPay. A DuitNow QR fee of RM1 (or 1%) is shown at checkout.

If a generation fails, the credit is returned once. Refund requests go through the support chat in the bot.
