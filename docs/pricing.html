Credits by duration

Clip length sets the credit cost. Output is 5 to 15 seconds, vertical 9:16 or landscape 16:9, 768P, generated by AI.

How many credits each clip length uses
Duration Credits Typical use
5 or 6 seconds 1 credit A short hook or single beat
10 seconds 2 credits A short product or scene clip with a little more room
15 seconds 3 credits A fuller clip with space for a call to action

How you pay

DuitNow QR and FPX

Payments are collected in Malaysian ringgit through ToyyibPay. You can pay with DuitNow QR or FPX bank transfer.

A DuitNow QR fee of RM1 (or 1%) is shown at checkout.

Order in the bot

Message @ZhongZena_bot, pick a pack, pay, then generate the clip in Telegram. Menus are in Bahasa Malaysia and English.

Support and follow-up also run through the same bot.

Telegram's own rules may change how digital goods are paid for. The bot is the source of truth at checkout.

Common questions

Do credits expire?

This page does not list a credit expiry date. Live terms are shown in the Telegram bot when you check out.

Can I top up later?

Yes. Buy another pack in the bot whenever you need more credits.

What happens if generation fails?

A failed job is refunded once.

Do I pay per video or subscribe?

There is no subscription. You buy credit packs when you need them. Clip length sets how many credits a job uses: 1 credit for 5 or 6 seconds, 2 credits for 10 seconds, and 3 credits for 15 seconds.
