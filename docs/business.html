Turn one product photo into a short ad clip

Made for Malaysian small businesses. Send one product photo and a short motion brief in Telegram. GENVID returns a 5 to 15 second AI video.

How a business order works

  1. Send one product photo

    Business customers start with a single product photo in the Telegram bot.

  2. Write a short brief

    Add a motion or dialogue brief, up to 1000 characters. Keep it plain and specific.

  3. Choose duration

    Pick 5, 6, 10, or 15 seconds. Credit cost follows clip length.

  4. Confirm and receive

    Confirm the job in the bot and receive the clip there when it is ready.

Honest beta notes

The business image-to-video path is in beta. Output is 720p and follows the source photo shape.

Exact text rendered inside a video is not guaranteed. Product, packaging, and logo fidelity are best effort.

Every delivered video is labelled AI-generated. GENVID asks buyers to keep that disclosure when they post the clip.

Credits and packs

Business jobs use the same credit maths as every other GENVID clip.

  • 1 credit = one 5 or 6 second clip
  • 2 credits = one 10 second clip
  • 3 credits = one 15 second clip

Packs start at RM5 for 1 credit. RM12 grants 3 credits. RM40 grants 12 credits (10 plus 2 bonus). RM90 grants 30 credits (25 plus 5 bonus). Full pack cards and payment detail sit on the pricing page.

Founding merchants can ask in the bot for the free managed business brief.

Pay in Malaysian ringgit with DuitNow QR or FPX through ToyyibPay. A DuitNow QR fee of RM1 (or 1%) is shown at checkout. The bot is the source of truth at checkout.
